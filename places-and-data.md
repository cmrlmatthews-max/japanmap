# Japan 3D Map: Places and Data (Fall 2027 Honeymoon)

Scope: 10-day trip. Tokyo, Kyoto, Nara, Osaka, Hakone, Mount Fuji area (Fujiyoshida / Kawaguchiko).

Notes on this file:
- Coordinates are WGS84, 4 decimals.
- Rows marked **approx** need a map check before use.
- Status: **core** = must-see classic. **suggestion** = lesser-known pick.
- Price tiers for ryokan are rough, from general knowledge. Confirm rates before booking.
